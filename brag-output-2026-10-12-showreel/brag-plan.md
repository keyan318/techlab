# Brag Plan: TechLab — Showreel Cut

## What is this app?
TechLab teaches programming, networking and cybersecurity through short lessons, in-browser labs and Astro, an AI tutor, all wrapped in a game (XP, streaks, gems, planets).

## The angle
A motion-designer's showreel where the product *is* the footage. Every scene is a real TechLab screen, captured from a local copy of the app with a fictional student ("NovaByte"), and the motion around it (3D device frames, camera push-ins, kinetic type, light sweeps, packet trails, beat-locked cuts) makes it feel like a launch trailer. The VO sets the pace; the three lab cuts land on the music's strongest beats.

## Hook (0–2.25s)
Black space. The four arms of the real pinwheel logo (#73b6ff, traced from `public/apple-touch-icon.png`) fly in from four directions and lock together with a flash and shockwave as the VO says "This is TechLab." The wordmark rises out of a mask, then the camera dives through the logo into the next scene.

## Key moments
- Three planets: the real planet art (Programming City, Networking Nebula, Cybersecurity Citadel) flips in on "code", "networks", "cyber defense".
- Rewards: the real progress screen (2,300 XP, 6-day streak, 4 gems) floats in a tilted 3D window; the real XP bolt / streak flame / gem icons from the landing page pop on "XP", "streaks", "gems", each ringing its stat in the screenshot. XP counter climbs to 2,300.
- Astro: "Stuck?" shakes on screen; pixel Astro (real 20x19 sprite from `astro-mascot.blade.php`) bounces in and talks; the real chat screen slides in and Astro's answer streams in.
- Labs, one per planet, hard cuts on strong beats: C editor printing `Diagnostics: PASS` + "You got it!", NetSim lab with packets running astro → relay → rivet and "Lab complete", Citadel Sim with "HIDDEN ACCOUNT FOUND" turning into "ALL CLEAR · Attack repelled".

## Outro / punchline
The landing hero, rebuilt: "Level up your" + the blue pill cycling Coding → Networking → Cybersecurity. Astro's glove cursor presses "Start your mission" on a strong beat, then the end card: logo, TechLab, Astro.

## User flow worth showing
Pick a planet → learn and earn XP → ask Astro when stuck → do the hands-on lab (Code / Configure / Defend it yourself) → lab complete.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic, with app-store polish
- Creative direction: "motion designer's showreel" — dense, kinetic, premium; real UI as footage
- Interpretation: fast entrances (0.3–0.5s), hard beat-locked cuts, camera moves on every screen, but every line holds long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #06061a deep space (landing page body), purple nebula glow; light app screens as the footage
- Accent: brand blue #2f7de1, logo blue #73b6ff, planet accents #73b6ff / #5be1ff / #9b6bff, XP gold #ffcf3f, success green
- Text: #eaeeff on dark, #1b1750 navy outlines (Astro)
- Display font: Space Grotesk 700 (landing/app headings); Pixelify Sans for pixel headings (dashboard); Inter body; Space Mono for labels
- Strongest visual element: pinwheel logo, Astro sprite, pixel planet art, real lab screens

## Share copy (draft)
TechLab: three planets, one AI tutor named Astro, and real labs where you write the code, wire the network and stop the attack.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support under a VO
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves vol 1 (120 BPM), media start 1.02s so beats fall on every 0.5s of video time
- Music treatment: bed at ~0.3 under the VO, lifts to ~0.55 after the last line (22.4s), fades out over the last 1.8s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset. Strong cues (track time 16.02, 17.02, 18.02, 20.02, 21.01, 22.01, 23.02) map to video 15.0, 16.0, 17.0, 19.0, 20.0, 21.0, 22.0 → "FOR REAL" slam 15.0, code cut 16.0, network cut 17.0, cyber cut 18.0 (beat), pill cycle 20.0/20.5/21.0, button press 22.0.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; nebula glow and logo halo breathe on the 0.5s beat grid. No visualizers.
- SFX posture: moderate-dense, motion-matched: synthesized whooshes on camera moves, a riser into "FOR REAL", soft impacts on slams, card slides on planet cards, pops for icons, chip stacks for the XP count, key ticks, bells on lab passes, one comedic buzz on "Stuck?".
- Restraint rule: SFX sit under the VO; nothing sharp repeats fast; no SFX on top of a VO consonant-heavy phrase louder than the voice.
